# Bistro

A co-op cooking game of Banana's own, on the Apps tile. The kitchen is on the top screen, with the dining room along
its top: customers come in by the left door, sit with their dish in a bubble, and leave by the right door, with
hearts when they're served or cross when they waited too long. The orders are on the bottom screen. Take food from
the crates, chop it, cook it, put it on a plate and serve it at the yellow counter.

| Mode | How it goes |
|---|---|
| Play | No clock: keep the customers happy. Each customer who leaves without their dish takes one of your three hearts, and losing the last one closes the bistro. Every eighth customer served gives a heart back. It gets busier the longer it lasts. Leaving (or closing the game) keeps the kitchen, and CONTINUE picks it up again. |
| Rush | Three minutes, as many tips as you can (stars at 40, 90 and 140). |
| Tutorial | 19 short lessons, one at a time: walking, taking, chopping, plates, each recipe, serving, dashing, two chefs, burning and the bin. The squares to go to light up, with an arrow. Customers wait and nothing burns. SELECT (or SKIP LESSON) skips a lesson. |

| Recipe | Made from |
|---|---|
| Salad | a chopped tomato and chopped lettuce, on a plate |
| Tomato soup | three chopped tomatoes cooked in a pot, scooped with a plate |
| Cheeseburger | bread, chopped meat fried in a pan, chopped cheese, on a plate |

Pots and pans burn if they're left too long (a red "!" warns first). Burnt food goes in the bin.

**Buttons:** D-pad walk, A take / put down, X chop (hold, at a board), B dash, Y switch chef, START pause. On the
bottom screen, the chef chips and PAUSE can be tapped, as can every menu button. Options: music and sounds, on or off.

## Files

- `bistro-game.h`: the rules (no drawing and no device), and a Play kitchen saved as text.
- `bistro-tutorial.h`: the tutorial's lessons and when each is done.
- `bistro-test.cpp`: plays the rules, and plays the whole tutorial through by walking to each square.
- `bistro.cpp`: the drawing, the screens, the customers, and the device: both panels by DRM page flips, the pad and
  the touchscreen by evdev (Activity's shim), and the sound and music by ALSA.
- `bistro-assets.h`, `bistro-sounds.h`: generated by `tools/bake.py` and `tools/bake-sounds.py` from Kenney's Food
  Kit, Emotes Pack, Kenney Future, Inter, Kenney's Interface Sounds, and Doge's "Catchy Swing" (CC0 except Inter, OFL;
  see THIRD_PARTY.md). The music goes in as 4-bit IMA ADPCM, decoded as it plays.
- `mockups/`: the game's own screens, drawn by `--smoke`.

## Build and test

The image builds it in `buildroot/package/banana-ui` (`-lasound`). Here, `make test` runs the rules' test and
draws each screen to `out/screen-*.png` (on macOS too: no device code). `banana-bistro --bench` reports what a frame
costs to draw. On the handheld, `run-bistro` starts it and keeps its files in `/data/apps/bistro`: `best.txt`,
`options.txt`, `save.txt` (a Play kitchen). The log, `/data/banana/logs/bistro.log`, says whether the sound device
opened, and why not if it didn't.

Re-baking (the packs in BananaLinux-PRs/cook-assets/raw):

    tools/bake.py <food-kit>/Previews "<ui-pack>/Font/Kenney Future.ttf" Inter-SemiBold.ttf Inter-Bold.ttf \
        bistro-assets.h "<emotes-pack>/PNG/Vector/Style 3"
    tools/bake-sounds.py <interface-sounds>/Audio bistro-sounds.h catchyswing.ogg
