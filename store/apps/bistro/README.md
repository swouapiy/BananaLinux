# Banana Bistro

A co-op cooking game of Banana's own, on the Apps tile. The kitchen is on the top screen and the orders on the bottom
one. Take food from the crates, chop it, cook it, put it on a plate and serve it at the yellow counter before the
order's time runs out. Tips earn stars (40, 90 and 140 in a three-minute round).

**First version:** one kitchen and three recipes. On one handheld you run two chefs, and Y switches between them.
Play together (one chef per handheld) comes next.

| Recipe | Made from |
|---|---|
| Salad | a chopped tomato and chopped lettuce, on a plate |
| Tomato soup | three chopped tomatoes cooked in a pot, scooped with a plate |
| Cheeseburger | bread, chopped meat fried in a pan, chopped cheese, on a plate |

Pots and pans burn if they're left too long (a red "!" warns first). Burnt food goes in the bin.

**Buttons:** D-pad walk, A take / put down, X chop (hold, at a board), B dash, Y switch chef, START pause. The
bottom screen's buttons and chef chips can be tapped.

## Files

- `bistro-game.h`: the rules (no drawing and no device), played by `bistro-test.cpp`.
- `bistro.cpp`: the drawing, the screens, and the device: both panels by DRM page flips, the pad and the
  touchscreen by evdev (Activity's shim), and the sound by ALSA.
- `bistro-assets.h`, `bistro-sounds.h`: generated by `tools/bake.py` and `tools/bake-sounds.py` from Kenney's Food
  Kit, Kenney Future, Inter and Kenney's Interface Sounds (all CC0 except Inter, OFL; see THIRD_PARTY.md).
- `mockups/`: the game's own screens, drawn by `--smoke`.

## Build and test

The image builds it in `buildroot/package/banana-ui` (`-lasound`). Here, `make test` runs the rules' test and
draws each screen to `out/screen-*.png` (on macOS too: no device code). `banana-bistro --bench` reports what a frame
costs to draw. On the handheld, `run-bistro` starts it, keeps the best score in `/data/apps/bistro/best.txt` and
the log in `/data/banana/logs/bistro.log`.
